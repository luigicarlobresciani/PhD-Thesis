2. CORRECTIONS TO TABLES AND EQUATIONS

Table II (p. 9), column $\psi^2\varphi^2 D$:
The two-sided covariant derivative is misprinted in seven operators:
$O^{(1)}_{\varphi\ell}$, $O^{(3)}_{\varphi\ell}$, $O_{\varphi e}$, $O^{(1)}_{\varphi q}$, $O^{(3)}_{\varphi q}$, $O_{\varphi u}$ and $O_{\varphi d}$.
In each of them, the $D$ is printed above a double arrow placed on the
baseline, instead of the arrow being placed over the $D$. The entries
should read:
  - $(\varphi^\dagger i\overset{\leftrightarrow}{D}{}_\mu\varphi)$ in $O^{(1)}_{\varphi\ell}$, $O_{\varphi e}$, $O^{(1)}_{\varphi q}$, $O_{\varphi u}$ and $O_{\varphi d}$;
  - $(\varphi^\dagger i\overset{\leftrightarrow}{D}{}_\mu^I \varphi)$ in $O^{(3)}_{\varphi\ell}$ and $O^{(3)}_{\varphi q}$.
This matches Appendix A (lines 621–622) and the caption of Fig. 4. The
entry for $O_{\varphi ud}$ is correct.

Table III (p. 9):
In three entries the coefficient $\tfrac{1}{4}$ should read $\tfrac{1}{3}$:
  $[C_{ud}^{(1)}]_{prst} + \tfrac{1}{4}[C_{ud}^{(8)}]_{prst}$  →  $[C_{ud}^{(1)}]_{prst} + \tfrac{1}{3}[C_{ud}^{(8)}]_{prst}$
  $-[C_{qu}^{(1)}]_{prst} - \tfrac{1}{4}[C_{qu}^{(8)}]_{prst}$  →  $-[C_{qu}^{(1)}]_{prst} - \tfrac{1}{3}[C_{qu}^{(8)}]_{prst}$
  $-[C_{qd}^{(1)}]_{prst} - \tfrac{1}{4}[C_{qd}^{(8)}]_{prst}$  →  $-[C_{qd}^{(1)}]_{prst} - \tfrac{1}{3}[C_{qd}^{(8)}]_{prst}$
This is a typographical error in our manuscript; the figures and results
are unaffected.

Eq. (B1) (p. 8):
For consistency with Eq. (3), please replace $\Im$ with $\text{Im}$ in
both terms, so that they read
$\left(\text{Im}\,a^0_{\bar\alpha\beta}(s) - 3\,\text{Im}\,a^1_{\alpha\beta}(s)\right)$.
