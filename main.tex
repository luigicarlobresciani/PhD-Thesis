\section{Spinor-Helicity Formalism}
The spinor-helicity formalism exploits the fact that the proper orthochronous Lorentz group is
doubly covered by $SL(2,\mathbb C)$, i.e.,
\begin{equation}
    SO^+(1,3) \cong SL(2,\mathbb C)/\mathbb Z_2\,.
\end{equation}

Books, reviews, and lecture notes: \cite{Dixon:1996wi,Dixon:2013uaa,Mangano:1990by,Cheung:2017pzi,Peskin:2011in,Elvang:2013cua,Elvang:2015rqa,Henn:2014yza,Badger:2023eqz,Travaglini:2022uwo,Ellis:2011cr,Dixon:2011xs}.
