Each directed black arrow represents a non-vanishing contribution to the anomalous dimension, pointing from the source operator to the target operator. The thickness of the arrows corresponds to the leading perturbative order at which the mixing appears, with thicker arrows corresponding to lower-order contributions. The labels on the arrows specify the corresponding coupling dependence and contain hyperlinks to the sections where the corresponding anomalous dimensions are reported.\label{fig:graph}}
